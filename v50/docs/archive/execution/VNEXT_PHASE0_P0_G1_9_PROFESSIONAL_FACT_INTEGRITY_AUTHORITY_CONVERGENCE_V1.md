# P0-G1.9 Professional Fact Integrity & Authority Convergence

## Purpose

P0-G1.9 closes only the machine-side defects that could contaminate the formal blind benchmark:

1. mechanically decidable professional fact errors;
2. production, experimental and research authority leakage;
3. Guest and Member product capability leakage;
4. non-sealed harness reproducibility after those changes.

It is not a cognitive redesign, product expansion, cleanup campaign or professional quality claim.

## Frozen Boundaries

This slice does not modify:

- the LLM cognitive prompt;
- Pattern or Hypothesis protocols;
- Graph, Path, Role or Ablation algorithms;
- Mingli theory or weights;
- Abu intelligence or UI;
- sealed formal charts;
- human Expert Reference content;
- Frontier model selection.

Review may annotate a deterministic conflict and suppress a high-risk public projection. Review may not rewrite raw cognition, select a new hypothesis, repair a work path, replace a useful god or regenerate a domain judgment.

## Deliverables

### Professional Fact Integrity Auditor v1

The auditor emits `ProfessionalFactIssue` records with:

```yaml
issue_id:

```

The first contract covers element generation/control direction, stem polarity and element, branch element and hidden stems, ten-god mapping, pillar references, root facts and modeled branch relations. Questions, quotations, hypotheses, counterfactuals and timing conditions are not treated as natal assertions.

### Production Authority Manifest

Every principal capability has one authority status:

```text
production | experimental | research | deprecated | archived
```

Independent First Look accepts production-authority facts only. Experimental Graph, Path, Role and estimated sensitivity observations can appear only in the Challenge Pack and carry `authority_status=experimental`.

### Public Capability Boundary

Guest and Member expose only:

```text
whole_chart
career
wealth
```

Practitioner and Research access remains a separate policy. The server rejects closed-domain exploration and Probe requests before any LLM call. Public role projection recursively removes research-only fields.

### Non-sealed Preflight

The preflight uses only the Development Set and Development Fixture Pack. It plans all six frozen lanes, exercises checkpoint recovery, hashes machine assets and verifies zero access to formal manifests, Expert Reference, Reality Evidence and the full taxonomy.
