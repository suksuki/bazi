# V50 Abu Dual-Lens Mingli Experience v1

Status: implementation authority

## Product promise

The user meets Abu, not two engines and not two reports.

```text
Birth confirmation
-> Bazi first look
-> Ziwei first look
-> What both lenses support
-> Where they differ
-> Abu's current whole-person understanding
-> One question that can distinguish the competing explanations
```

## Lens responsibilities

```text
Bazi
  long-term structure, body/function, work path, capacity and failure conditions

Ziwei
  life arena, palace expression, role emphasis, four transformations and time-window context

Abu synthesis
  agreement, tension, current interpretation, uncertainty and next question
```

Ziwei never overwrites Bazi facts. Bazi never invents palaces or stars. Agreement
may strengthen a case hypothesis; disagreement must remain visible and generate a
question rather than being averaged away.

## Ziwei source quality

The previous simplified star-placement seed is not authorized for user-facing
judgment. The production candidate uses the pinned open-source `iztro` chart
calculator and records its version and warnings.

Ziwei may participate only when:

```text
birth date is confirmed
birth time is exact or named two-hour branch
gender is confirmed
chart calculator succeeds
12 palaces and required identity facts are present
```

Approximate or unknown birth time produces a visible boundary and a time-focused
clarification question. It does not produce a confident Ziwei reading.

## Abu rhythm

Abu speaks once per accepted artifact:

```text
1. 命盘事实已经确认。
2. 八字第一眼已经形成，但我还在比较替代解释。
3. 紫微第一眼已经形成，我现在比较两张盘看到的是不是同一件事。
4. 两个视角共同支持……；它们在……上仍有差异。
5. 回答这一个问题，可以区分长期方向与当前角色。
```

No stage may be simulated by a timer and no failed Ziwei calculation may be
replaced with generic astrology prose.

## Canvas

The main canvas has four user-facing views:

```text
综合  Abu's current understanding
八字  structure and work path
紫微  palace and star expression
为什么 evidence, tension and unknowns
```

Guest sees one shared recognition point and one boundary. Member sees the full
synthesis and probe. Practitioner sees both lenses, competing explanations and
source quality. Research sees raw references and rejected alternatives.

## Validation

Validation must include:

```text
different day structures
different birth-hour branches
same day with changed hour
solar/lunar equivalent inputs
male/female direction-sensitive cases
unknown-time boundary
Bazi/Ziwei agreement
Bazi/Ziwei tension
```

Success means Ziwei contributes a distinct observation or a useful tension. A
second paraphrase of the Bazi reading is a failure.
