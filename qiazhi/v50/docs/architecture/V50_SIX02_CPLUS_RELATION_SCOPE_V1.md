# SIX-02-01 C+ Relation Scope Baseline

Status: implemented baseline

## Decision

1. Five-element generation and control describe a potential relation. They do not, by themselves, prove direct action.
2. The complete potential field remains available to practitioner and Lab projections. It is excluded from the ordinary action graph and default member projection.
3. Same-pillar placement records bearing and proximity. It does not make a stem act directly on a whole branch.
4. Cross-level action requires a named mechanism, such as hidden-stem participation, rooting, emergence, same-pillar bearing, transformation, or approved temporal activation.
5. A branch is not reduced to one dominant element. Cross-level candidates target its concrete hidden stems or an explicitly approved whole-branch state.
6. Natal, luck, and annual layers share one relation ontology. Temporal entry changes relation state; it does not create a second ontology.

## State Chain

```text
potential -> structural -> time_activated -> effective
```

- `potential`: an observable candidate in the full research field; not path-qualified.
- `structural`: a named mechanism is present in the chart structure.
- `time_activated`: an approved temporal relation has entered the current scene.
- `effective`: a committed LifeCase assertion says the mechanism actually participates.

State progression is not automatic. Each transition requires evidence and a named mechanism.

## Example

For `甲戌`, the formal graph must not create a vague `甲克戌` action edge. The Lab potential field may retain independent atomic candidates between `甲` and the hidden stems `戊`, `辛`, and `丁`. A later formal assertion must name the mechanism and its target.

## Locked Synthetic Groups

The fixture `tests/fixtures/v50_six02_cplus_relation_scope_v1.json` locks three discriminating groups:

1. Same-pillar bearing and branch hidden-stem decomposition.
2. Repeated labels in different pillar slots, proving stable positional identity.
3. Natal, luck, and annual projection, proving that generic element relations stay potential while named temporal relations become activated.

## Projection Boundary

- Ordinary member UI: structural, time-activated, and effective relations only.
- Practitioner and Lab: may request the potential field and hidden-stem detail.
- Reasoner, Canvas, Abu, Theater, Xiangfa, and Workspace do not promote potential relations.
- A projection may hide information but must never recreate filtered relations through fallback logic.

## Deferred Questions

This baseline does not decide relation priority, transformation success, clash resolution, combination qualification, path scoring, or other remaining SIX-02 semantic questions. Those require separate candidate rules, discriminating fixtures, and explicit approval.
