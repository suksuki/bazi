# V60 Mingli Core and Quantitative Model

Status: `EVIDENCE_PREPARATION_REQUEST_V1_IMPLEMENTED_AND_REAL_BROWSER_AUDITED_LOCAL`

## Product decision

Mingli Calculation is the permanent core of Abu Knows. Dream, Lab, Abu Says
and Theater consume its versioned outputs; none of them may create a second
chart, fact chain or verdict.

The product objective is not to make traditional terms look numerical. It is
to improve decision quality through:

1. exact deterministic calculation;
2. explicit evidence and competing interpretations;
3. measurable uncertainty;
4. calibration against authorized real outcomes;
5. bounded LLM comparison only where rules cannot select safely.

V60 does not currently have a defensible empirical fortune-reading success
rate. Synthetic fixtures, expert-authored examples and uncalibrated scores
must not be reported as real-world accuracy.

```text

-> SYSTEM

-> RULE_ENGINE
```

## Quantitative Mingli model

V60 separates four numerical meanings.

### 1. Deterministic measurements

These are reproducible chart measurements, not probabilities:

- five-element composition and flow vector;
- yin-yang distribution;
- Ten-God presence, manifestation and carrier matrix;
- root/source evidence states;
- typed relation and graph structure;
- candidate-path continuity, blockers and shared participants;
- timing-layer deltas;
- evidence coverage and contradiction counts.

Every measurement binds a chart version, profile version, algorithm version,
source references and replay Hash.

### 2. Evidence support scores

A score may compare qualified candidates:

```text
candidate support
= supporting evidence contributions
- counter-evidence
- unresolved-capacity penalty
- missing-data penalty
- source-quality penalty
```

Until calibrated, it must be labelled `SUPPORT_SCORE_NOT_PROBABILITY`.
Weight changes create a new immutable `QuantProfile`; they never rewrite an
old Reading.

### 3. Calibrated probabilities

Only predictions with authorized, time-stamped and independently resolved
outcomes may train or calibrate a probability model. A probability output must
carry:

- sample size and population definition;
- domain and outcome window;
- model/profile version;
- confidence interval or uncertainty band;
- calibration status;
- evidence-quality distribution;
- abstention conditions.

Before the minimum data and calibration criteria are met, the Runtime reports
`NOT_CALIBRATED` rather than a percentage.

### 4. Decision-quality evaluation

Lab evaluates more than raw accuracy:

- Brier score and log loss;
- calibration curve and expected calibration error;
- coverage and abstention rate;
- confusion by outcome class;
- performance by domain, rule profile, timing window and evidence quality;
- drift, school-profile disagreement and data leakage;
- real-human evidence separated from synthetic simulation.

Synthetic NPCs can test mechanics and closed-world consistency. They cannot
establish real-world Mingli validity.

## Inspectable explanation contract

The product does not equate a confident sentence with an accurate reading.
Every displayed claim is projected by Mingli into one versioned explanation:

```text
claim
-> supporting evidence
-> admitted counter-evidence or NOT_ADMITTED
-> unresolved questions
-> professional and predictive boundary
```

The claim status is always one of:

```text
CONFIRMED
CANDIDATE
OBSERVE
```

`CONFIRMED` is reserved for deterministic chart facts under an active Profile.
`CANDIDATE` means the visible structure deserves comparison, not that it is
effective work. `OBSERVE` is a bounded real-life attention window, not a
prediction. Absence of an admitted counter-model is never counted as support.

## Mechanism evidence-completeness contract

V60 now projects one immutable qualification matrix from the same Reading,
quantitative, mechanism and timing vectors:

```text
structural roles
source / manifestation
timing overlap
counter-evidence
effect
capacity
usability
professional admission
```

The matrix is not a score. Each dimension exposes its current status, bound
evidence, required next evidence and falsifier. `PRESENT` and `PARTIAL` mean
only that inspectable material exists. They do not contribute points and do
not imply effective work. Effect, capacity, usability and professional
admission remain `NOT_ADMITTED` until separately versioned rules and evidence
are approved.

Mingli Calculation, Lab and Abu consume the same qualification Ref and Hash.
The browser cannot add missing evidence, infer a professional verdict or turn
the matrix into a probability. Mixed Case, Chart or source-vector lineage
fails closed. A read-only corpus test currently covers four persisted real
owner Cases and confirms deterministic but case-sensitive projections.

The matrix is displayed beside the attention Decision after the Decision has
been read. It is not currently an input to that static natal comparison and
cannot be used to reinterpret its recorded rationale as professional
selection.
