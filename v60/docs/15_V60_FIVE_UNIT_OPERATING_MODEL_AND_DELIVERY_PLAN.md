# V60 Five-Unit Operating Model And Delivery Plan

Status: `ACTIVE_PRODUCT_AUTHORITY`

## Product Definition

V60 is a Mingli-centered Life Intelligence product with Abu Dream World as
its priority product breakthrough.

```text
Mingli Calculation = the core brain and truth-facing product
Mingli Lab         = the research, experiment and improvement loop
Abu Says           = the native explanation and interaction layer of Mingli
Abu Dream          = the persistent game that makes uncertainty verifiable
Abu Theater        = a relatively independent directing and media studio
```

The five units are not five equal applications and must not build five copies
of Case, facts, reasoning, story or progress.

## Product Priority

| Priority | Unit | Product responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Mingli Calculation | Calculate, qualify and preserve formal readings |
| 2 | Abu Dream | Turn bounded uncertainty into play, waiting and evidence |
| 3 | Mingli Lab | Research rules, compare mechanisms and improve Mingli |
| 4 | Abu Says | Explain the current Mingli result in human language |
| 5 | Abu Theater | Direct and distribute approved stories and scenes |

Dream remains the largest unfinished V60 experience. This does not make Dream
the truth owner. A Dream question, visual response or player answer cannot
create Mingli truth.

## One Shared Life Proposition

Every integrated slice begins with one versioned Life Proposition:

```text
Case and LifeCase revision
-> admitted facts and knowledge profile
-> formal or unresolved Mingli proposition
-> competing interpretations and evidence boundary
-> user-facing expression
-> optional Dream judgment and independent World evidence
-> Reveal and reconciliation
```

All online units must bind the same:

```text
case_ref
chart_version_ref
life_case_revision_ref
knowledge_profile_ref + version + hash
fact_refs
candidate_refs
decision_ref when formally admitted
cutoff and disclosure boundary
```

Copying prose between units without this shared identity is not integration.

## Mingli Calculation Core

Mingli owns:

- deterministic chart and formal facts;
- LifeCase revision and evidence manifest;
- admitted rule execution;
- formal reading identity and reproducibility;
- explicit unresolved and abstention states.

Mingli must never silently upgrade a Lab hypothesis, an LLM answer, a Dream
choice or a Theater story into a formal conclusion.

The first executable shared object is a versioned `MingliReadingEnvelope`.
It records exact source versions, facts, admitted decisions, unresolved
dimensions and the knowledge profile used by that calculation.

## Lab As A Live Research Loop

Lab is not an isolated graph viewer. Its operating loop is:

```text
candidate rule or mechanism
-> bounded experiment and comparison
-> evidence result: supported / weakened / unresolved
-> admission decision
-> new immutable Knowledge or Rule Profile version
-> new Mingli calculations use the admitted version immediately
```

“Immediately” means immediately after formal admission, not before it.

- New calculations resolve the newest admitted compatible Profile.
- Existing readings remain pinned to their original Profile and remain
  reproducible.
- Clear mechanical admission may be decided by the system.
- Professional controversy, insufficient evidence or irreversible meaning
  remains unresolved or requires Owner professional review.
- An LLM may compare admitted candidates, but cannot publish a rule version.

Lab results that are not admitted remain visible research and have no effect
on formal Mingli output.

## Abu Says Is Native To Mingli

Abu Says consumes the exact `MingliReadingEnvelope`; it does not perform a
second chart reading.

Every formal Mingli result can expose Abu in place:

```text
what is known
why it is known
what remains unresolved
what evidence could change the reading
what deserves attention next
```

The dedicated Abu surface may preserve conversations, topics and continuity,
but every claim must retain the source Reading and evidence references. Abu
may clarify user meaning and collect observations. Abu cannot create facts,
select an effective work path or overwrite the Reading.

## Dream As The Priority Game

Dream consumes a bounded unresolved Life Proposition and turns it into:

```text
encounter
-> observation
-> meaningful choice
-> immutable judgment
-> independent World development
-> evidence
-> Reveal
-> reconciliation
```

Dream is successful only when it is playable and creates a reason to return.
It is not successful merely because the database can create Encounters.

Dream learning returns as an Observation or Evaluation reference. It cannot
change Mingli truth until a separately admitted research process qualifies it.

Dream may also preserve one account-private reality question and later
self-reports:

```text
user-selected domain + private short question
-> exact-domain synthetic life, without text interpretation
-> independent Dream judgment and World result
-> one server-issued seven-day reality observation
-> append-only Grove check-ins
```

This is a personal reflection loop, not the professional research process.
The question does not alter Dream canon; the Dream result does not answer it;
the observation and check-ins are `NOT_MINGLI_EVIDENCE` and cannot update the
Reading, Decision ledger or Knowledge.

## Theater As A Relatively Independent Studio

Theater is not part of the online Mingli decision chain. It is a directing and
media-production consumer of approved source material.

```text
committed Mingli / World / Story source
-> Director Brief
-> Script and dialogue
-> Storyboard and shot list
-> voice, music and sound plan
-> media composition
-> in-product scene / YouTube / Douyin / other channel package
```

Every production package must declare:

- `CANONICAL_REPLAY`, `AUTHORIZED_ADAPTATION`, `SIMULATION_BRANCH` or
  `FICTIONAL_EDUCATION`;
- the exact source facts, events, decisions and disclosure rights;
- script, storyboard, voice, asset and render versions;
- target aspect ratio, language and distribution channel;
- whether the output may return to the product as an in-system scene.

Direction, dialogue, voice and editing may transform expression. They cannot
change the underlying event or Mingli conclusion.

## V50 Reuse Policy For The Mingli Core

V50 remains a read-only source. V60 does not import its Runtime.

| V50 capability | V60 disposition |
| --- | --- |
| deterministic pillars and bounded foundation maps | already reimplemented and admitted |
| typed Fact, RelationAssertion and PathAssertion contracts | transform and selectively reimplement |
| source and manifestation evidence profile | research reference only until V60 admission |
| path qualification and provenance checks | selectively reimplement before effective-work claims |
| Life Domain protocols | transform into versioned V60 reading topics |
| natural-language path prose and benchmark reports | reference only, never Runtime truth |
| old Dream providers, fallbacks and page state | rejected |

The next migration candidate must improve one visible Mingli reading. It may
not be admitted merely because V50 contains code or a report.

## Delivery Cadence

Each product iteration uses both a vertical and horizontal test:

1. Vertical: one LifeCase and one proposition travel end to end.
2. Horizontal: only the proven rule, contract or UI pattern becomes reusable.
3. Product: the user sees a meaningful change.
4. Professional: sources, uncertainty and abstention remain honest.
5. Recovery: refresh and process restart preserve the same versioned result.

## Active Delivery Plan

### Slice 1: Shared Mingli Reading — `IMPLEMENTED_AND_PERSISTED_LOCAL`

- Build one versioned `MingliReadingEnvelope`.
- Bind the current Home Case to exact Knowledge and candidate-rule Profiles.
- Integrate Abu's explanation inside the Mingli result.
- Make Lab show the same Reading identity and research boundary.
- Persist each Reading append-only with exact Profile hashes, so historical
  results remain replayable after future admitted Profile changes.

### Slice 2: First V50 Capability Admission

- Use one explicit, hash-locked active Profile selection for new readings.
- Select one V50 capability that improves a real Reading.
- Reimplement it behind a V60 contract and tests.
- Keep it research-only until its source and professional boundary qualify.
- Prove a newly admitted version affects new readings without rewriting old
  readings.

The activation and historical-replay mechanism is implemented. No additional
V50 capability has been professionally admitted yet.

### Slice 3: First Integrated Life Proposition

- Use one authorized real LifeCase and one narrow, reviewable proposition.
- Show facts and competing interpretations in Mingli and Lab.
- Let Abu explain the same proposition without adding a conclusion.
- Carry only the bounded unknown into Dream.

### Slice 4: Dream Return Value — `PARTIALLY_IMPLEMENTED_IN_DREAM`

- The playable judgment and independent World-evidence loop is complete.
- An optional private reality question can now enter the exact-domain Dream
  life without selecting or changing its Story.
- A completed, reconciled journey exposes three server-issued observation
  options, persists one seven-day task and accepts append-only self-reported
  follow-ups after Grove return.
- These records bind the current Owner Case/Reading for provenance but remain
  inside the private Dream ledger and outside chart truth.
- Returning a qualified Observation Seed into the private Home LifeTree is
  still not implemented. No current Dream task or check-in may be presented as
  that seed.

### Slice 5: Theater Studio Package

- Compile one committed story into a Director Brief, script, storyboard,
  voice plan and multi-channel delivery manifest.
- Reuse the versioned V60 media library.
- Do not make Theater a second Story or truth engine.

## Current Exclusions

- no mobile visual expansion;
- no multilingual implementation, only language-neutral identities;
- no bulk NPC or question factory;
- no automatic self-training;
- no automatic semantic evaluation of private questions or self-reported
  check-ins;
- no production deployment or Server 13 synchronization;
- no claim of Mingli accuracy before blinded real-case evidence exists.
